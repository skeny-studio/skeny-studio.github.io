Delete Your Skeny Attendance Account

You can ask us to delete your Skeny Attendance account at any time. Send your request by email and we will take care of the rest.

Email your request to skenystudio@gmail.com

Send deletion request

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to skenystudio@gmail.com with the subject “Skeny Attendance Account Deletion Request”.
  2. In the message, include the email address or account name you use in the app, so we can find your account.
  3. We will process your request and confirm by email.

What happens after we receive your request

  • Your account association is removed.
  • No personal data is retained on our servers.
  • Requests are processed within 7 days.

Data stored on your device

Attendance records and biometric data stored locally on your device are not held on our servers. You can delete them yourself by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s data:

  • Android: Settings › Apps › Skeny Attendance › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Data you have already exported or shared with other apps, such as Excel or PDF reports, is not deleted by this process.
